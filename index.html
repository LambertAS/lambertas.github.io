Intro

I finished a Computer Science and Statistics degree at BINUS this year and spent the last eight months as a data analyst intern in HR information systems at PT Lion Super Indo, a national supermarket chain.

Most of what I do sits in the gap between a business question and a working system. Someone in HR needs to know something. The data exists, but it is spread across exports that do not share a key, and the question they asked is not quite the question the data can answer. The work is figuring out what they actually need, whether the data can support it, and then building something that keeps producing the answer after I stop touching it.

My degree was half statistics, which shapes how I approach this. I am more interested in whether a number is trustworthy than in whether it is impressive.
